Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 15, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using our application, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, and password when you register.
  • Expense data: the expenses you record, including amounts, categories, dates, and notes.
  • Usage information: basic technical details such as browser type, device information, and pages visited, used to keep the service running smoothly.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Store, organise, and display your expenses and spending summaries.
  • Maintain, secure, and improve the application.
  • Communicate with you about your account or important service updates.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share information only:

  • With service providers who help us operate the application, under obligations to keep it confidential.
  • When required by law, regulation, or a valid legal request.
  • To protect the rights, safety, and security of our users and the application.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. However, no method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in and to remember your preferences. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but some features of the application may not work correctly without them.

6. Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymise your personal data, unless we are required to retain it for legal reasons.

7. Your Rights & Choices

  • Access and review the personal information we hold about you.
  • Update or correct inaccurate account details.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us at support@example.com.
